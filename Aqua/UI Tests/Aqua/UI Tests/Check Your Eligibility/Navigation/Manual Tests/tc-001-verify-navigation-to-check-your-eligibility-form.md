# TC-001 — Verify navigation to 'Check your eligibility' form

**Priority:** HIGH

## Preconditions
Navigate to https://www.aquacard.co.uk/credit-cards

## Steps
1. Click on ‘Check my eligibility’ button
2. Verify that the URL begins with https://www.aquacard.co.uk/aqua/online/classic/quote/

## Expected Result
The ‘Check your eligibility’ form is displayed with the correct URL.
